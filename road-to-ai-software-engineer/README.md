<summary>Difference between AI and Machine Learning</summary>

✅ **Artificial Intelligence (AI) :** AI is the broader concept of machines being able to perform tasks that typically require **human intelligence**, like reasoning, learning, problem-solving, or understanding language.

**Example:**

* A **self-driving car** that can navigate traffic, recognize stop signs, and make decisions is using AI.
* **ChatGPT**: Understands and responds like a human — that's AI.

✅ **Machine Learning (ML):** ML is a **subset of AI** that uses **data and algorithms** to **learn patterns** and make decisions **without being explicitly programmed**.

**Example:**

* A **spam filter** that learns from past emails to classify new ones as spam or not.
* **Netflix recommendations** based on what you watched before — that’s ML in action.

***

* **AI is the goal**, ML is one way to **achieve that goal**.
* All ML is AI, but **not all AI is ML**.
* **AI** = A robot that acts like a human. **ML** = Teaching that robot how to play chess by letting it study thousands of games, instead of coding every possible move.

</details>
